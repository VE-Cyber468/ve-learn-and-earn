Money Skills: Understanding Your Electricity Bill

Electricity bills can contain unfamiliar words, charges and figures. In this lesson, you'll learn what those details mean and how to check them.

By the end, you'll be able to:

  • Recognise the important parts of a bill.
  • Understand units, standing charges and VAT.
  • Check how the total is calculated.
  • Identify when to ask your supplier a question.

1. Learn the basics

An electricity bill usually includes two main types of charges.

⚡ Unit rate

The price you pay for each kilowatt-hour (kWh) of electricity you use.

Example: 25p per kWh.

📅 Standing charge

A fixed daily charge that may apply even when you use very little electricity.

Example: 50p per day.

What is VAT?

VAT is a tax. Domestic electricity is generally charged at a reduced VAT rate of 5% in the UK.
